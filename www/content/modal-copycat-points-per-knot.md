Copycat fits a smoothing spline (via a GAM) to each historical season's weekly growth rate before using it as a matching template and forecast generator. **Data Points per Knot** controls how many weeks of data the spline uses per knot — fewer points per knot means a more flexible, wigglier fit that follows short-term ups and downs more closely; more points per knot means a smoother fit that follows the broad seasonal shape but averages over week-to-week noise.

This is now a value you can adjust and compare (it used to be a fixed setting). We'd suggest trying somewhere in the range of **3 to 6**, which is also why the input only accepts whole numbers in that range: much lower risks overfitting to noisy weekly data, and much higher can smooth away real features of the season's shape.
